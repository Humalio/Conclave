## Tern-9 night watch

Tern-9 runs two watches. The night watch is staffed by cryo technicians on rotation from Relay Engineering's [[Cryo plant]] team. **Bel Arkwright** is the night cryo technician who answered the dish alarm on 9 April and found the frost beside dish panel 7. She is not on any team list above because she is not on any team; she keeps the cold side cold. Her write-up is in [[IR-0412 Tern-9 cryo alarm]].

## Reporting lines and escalation

1. Team member → team lead.
2. Team lead → Saoirse.
3. Saoirse → I. Calloway.
4. Disclosure concerns go straight to Marguerite at any level. Nobody needs permission to talk to the Disclosure Board; see [[Independent review rules]].

Decisions that only the Director can make are listed as pending in the [[Decision log]]. As of 1 June there are four.
