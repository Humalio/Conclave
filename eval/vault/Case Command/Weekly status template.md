# Weekly status template

Copy this note for each weekly status report. Name the copy "Status YYYY-MM-DD (Team)". Submit by Monday 10:00 Corvel time; Case Command rolls the reports into the case summary by Monday 16:00 and Ilse forwards it to the Ministry on Tuesday.

Keep it short. A status report is not a lab notebook. If you need more than a page, you need a separate note and a link to it.

## How to fill it in

1. **Headline.** One sentence. What would you say if Saoirse caught you in the corridor?
2. **RAG.** Red, amber or green, against your team's next milestone in [[Milestones]]. Green means "on date with float". Amber means "on date, no float". Red means "not on date". There is no "greenish".
3. **Done this week.** Results only, each with a capture ID or a note link. R11 applies to status reports too: "we think" is not a result.
4. **Next week.** What you will finish, not what you will touch.
5. **Blockers.** Link the blocker note (see [[B-01 Firmware 7 fault]] for the format). If it is not written down, it is not blocking.
6. **Risks.** New or changed scores only. Add to your team register first; Case Command lifts anything 12+ into the [[Risk register]].
7. **Decisions needed.** Name the decider. If the decider is the Director, say so; the [[Decision log]] keeps a pending list.
8. **Anything odd.** See below.

Exposure is reported in echo-hours. Cite the capture ID for every timing. Do not write "glitch" in a report; the Ministry has asked, in writing.

## About "Anything odd"

This line was added in revision 2 of the template, after 14 March. It exists because the case nearly lost a real observation in an average.

Use it for anything that does not fit a category but that you would want to know about if you were in someone else's team: a reply ahead of its message, a log entry with the wrong timestamp, a tick in the dish house, a dream a keeper will not repeat, a number that is right in the summary and wrong in the raw file. Do not filter it. Do not explain it. Write what you saw and where the raw data is.

Examples from past reports (lightly edited):

- *Timekeepers, week ending 4 Apr:* "Summary shows no echo after 47. Raw chain shows echo 52: a reply, and no send on record. See [[Second timestamp]]."
- *Xenobiology, week ending 11 Apr:* "Night technician reports ticking from the F-3 cell when the dish is cold. Logged."
- *Signals Lab, week ending 7 Mar:* "Nothing odd." (This one is kept as an example of what not to write. Compare [[Lab notebook — Imani]] for the same week.)

## Distribution

Status reports are readable by all case staff. Do not paste content from Classified notes into a status report, even as a quotation; link to the public note instead and let the access rules do their work.
