# Case charter

> Find what is answering the Net before it answers, and keep the Net trusted while we do.

The Echo Case charter, revision C, issued 6 January 2191. Signed I. Calloway, Director, for the Halcyon Relay Authority. Countersigned Corin Ashdale, procurement liaison, for the Tern-9 refit contract.

## Purpose

The Meridian Net is forty relay stations strung from Mercury to the Kuiper edge. Every call, trade and heartbeat in the system passes through them. For a year, the furthest of them, Tern-9, has been logging replies that arrive before the message they answer has left the dish. The lead is always the same: 0.71 s. The first 46 were filed as clock noise. Echo 47 was not.

The Echo Case exists to find the cause, once, carefully, and without the Net losing the trust of the people who use it. It is run from the Annex, the Authority's Office of Anomalous Traffic: three desks in the basement of Corvel Station. Everything in this vault serves the sentence at the top of this page.

## Scope

**In scope**

- Signal physics sufficient to freeze an echo model ([[Echo model v1]] is the current baseline) and design the Tern-9 dish rebuild against it.
- The dish array, the shielding, the transmit power train and the relay clocks.
- Keeper protection against echo sleep, including shielded bunks and exposure limits ([[Exposure limit]]).
- The physical refit at Tern-9 ([[Refit plan]]).
- An independent disclosure case and order ([[Disclosure case]]).

**Out of scope**

- Any deliberate reply to the echo beyond the one scheduled for M7.
- Shutting down relays other than Tern-9 (the Authority will not fund a dark Net).
- Public statements about the echo, until M6 closes.

## Success criteria

1. All twelve requirements R1–R12 verified, each against a named capture ID (R11 insists on it).
2. Disclosure approved by the Disclosure Board (M6).
3. One deliberate reply sent and logged under controlled conditions (M7), with every keeper inside the exposure limit.
4. Tern-9 returned to ordinary relay service afterwards, which is why the refit must add no more than 6 % to station mass.

## Budget envelope

The Authority has committed funds through M4 (live listen, due 30 September). Release of the M5–M7 tranche is conditional on M4 passing on schedule. Case Command asks every team to remember this when they read the [[Risk register]]: a slip at M4 is not just a slip, it is a funding review.
