# Disclosure basis

The rules against which the Disclosure Board shall judge the Echo Case. Frozen 3 February 2191 by resolution DB-2191-02. Any change requires a two-thirds vote of the Board and a new revision letter.

## Purpose

1.1 Telling the Meridian Net that something is answering it would be a first. No prior standard exists for disclosing an unexplained signal source to a network that runs on trust. This basis therefore adopts the Net's staffed-relay safety standard, NSS-4, in full, and adds the echo-specific clauses in §3 below.

1.2 The disclosure order (milestone M6, due 31 Jan 2192) shall be issued only when every clause below is met or formally waived by the Board.

## Standards adopted

2.1 NSS-4 *Staffed relay safety*, all parts.

2.2 NSS-4 Part 7, *Cryogenic systems*, applies to the receiver cold face and the cryo plant.

2.3 NSS-4 Part 11, *High-energy storage*, applies to the capacitor bank and the dish feed modules.

2.4 Where NSS-4 is silent, the Board shall rule, and the ruling shall be added to this basis as a numbered clause.

## Echo-specific clauses

3.1 Every hazard shall be recorded in the [[Hazard log]] with cause, controls and verification.

3.2 Every safety-critical item shall receive independent review per [[Independent review rules]] (R10).

3.3 Every result used as evidence shall trace to a capture ID (R11).

3.4 The Board shall not accept a model as evidence for a regime in which the model has not been tested. Extrapolation beyond tested transmit power shall be stated as such in the [[Disclosure case]].

3.5 The Board shall not accept an observation as explained merely because it has not recurred.

3.6 No uncharacterised biological material shall be in the relay loop or in the keepers' quarters (R12; added by ruling DB-2191-09, 23 April).

Class 1 is intolerable. Class 2 is tolerable only with Board approval and a named owner. Classes 3 and 4 are acceptable with verification.
