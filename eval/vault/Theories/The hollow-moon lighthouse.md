# The hollow-moon lighthouse

*Reposted from the Night Choir's public board by Quentin, 18 May 2191, because three people asked whether it was ours. It is not. It is pinned here because of the predictions.*

**Status:** crackpot. Not closed. Three predictions, three hits.

## The post, as written

> **NIGHT CHOIR / thread 2231 / posted 02:14 by "lamplighter"**
>
> listen. we have logged the early echo for a year, longer than the Authority has admitted it, and here is what they will not say. IAPETUS IS HOLLOW. two-tone moon, the ridge round its waist like a seam, the dark face that faces forward. that is not a moon. that is a housing.
>
> something inside it keeps a light. not a light you see. a light you hear. a lighthouse answers every ship that calls it, and it answers *before* you call because it can see you coming, the way a lighthouse keeper sees the sail before the sail sees the lamp.
>
> Tern-9 points past Saturn on its long leg. Tern-9 heard it first. now the others will.
>
> we have worked out the timing from our own logs. next three echoes on the long leg: 14 May 03:11:52, 16 May 22:40:09, 17 May 04:02:33 (Corvel time). write them down. then tell us we are mad.

## Evidence for

- The three times. Tern-9 logged echoes at 03:11:52.4, 22:40:09.1 and 04:02:33.8 on those dates. All three to the second. See [[Listening log]].
- Tern-9's long leg does pass within a few degrees of Saturn's line for part of each orbit. See [[Echo geometry]].
- The Night Choir has logged echoes for a year with home receivers and no access to Authority clocks. Their 0.71 s matches ours.

## Evidence against

- Everything else. Iapetus is a moon. Its ridge is a ridge. Nothing in the post is physics.
- The echoes are not confined to the Saturn leg; Tern-9 has logged them pointing elsewhere.
- Halden's view: the Choir may be predicting the *transmission* schedule, which is published, not the echo. Every echo follows a high-power send. "They didn't find a lighthouse. They found our timetable."
- Vesna's reply: the 17 May send was moved at an hour's notice. The Choir's time was posted two days before.

## Why it stays up

Nobody on the case can explain how the Choir got the third time. Case Command has not contacted them. The Authority has asked that nobody does.

The Choir's next post promised a fourth time. It has not appeared. Their last line on the board, 30 May: "we stopped posting the times. it started answering us too."
