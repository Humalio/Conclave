# The dish is alive

*Filed by Xenobiology (Petra, countersigned Linnea). Ayo declined to sign and asked that this be recorded.*

**Status:** open. Half-supported. The empty-dish capture is the test.

## The theory

The frost on Tern-9's receiver face is not a contaminant that happens to sit on a dish. It is the thing doing the listening. It grows in filaments a few microns wide along the cold face, and filaments that fine, that regular, laid out on a parabola, are an antenna whether or not anyone meant them to be. It hears the reply first. We think it is learning to make it.

The echo started in the year the frost would have started growing. The frost gains mass only after high-power transmissions. The echo comes only after high-power transmissions. Two curves, one shape.

## Evidence for

- The frost ticks and re-forms about 0.7 s before each echo arrives. See [[Frost feeding log]].
- It was found next to dish panel 7, on the part of the dish that hears best. See [[IR-0412 Tern-9 cryo alarm]].
- On 11 April a keeper mapped faint frost at nine spots on the face. The spots are evenly spaced, as if placed.
- Signals Lab already treats it as an instrument. See [[Frost as an instrument]].
- Keepers who sleep near the dish hear the reply in their dreams before it arrives. Something near the dish is broadcasting it early.

## Evidence against

- Nobody has seen the frost produce a signal, only respond to one.
- [[Frost is a contaminant]]: Ayo's position is that it is feeding on the dish, not running it.
- The obvious test has been run: scrape the face, sterilise the dish, listen again. See [[CAP-014b Empty dish]].

## What would settle it

If the dish is alive, an empty dish should be deaf. If an empty dish still hears the reply 0.71 s early, the frost is a listener, not a speaker.

Petra's last line in the filing: "Then it is the only thing on Tern-9 that hears the answer coming, and we should ask it what it hears." Read [[CAP-014b Empty dish]] before you agree.
