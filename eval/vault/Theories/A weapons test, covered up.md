# A weapons test, covered up

*Filed by Halden, the Annex. Filed reluctantly, and only after Vesna said she would file it under his name if he did not.*

**Status:** open. Circumstantial. The author would like to be wrong.

## The theory

I was an Authority auditor for nine years. I know what the Authority's paperwork looks like when it is hiding a cost, and I know what it looks like when it is hiding an event. This is the second kind.

Something was tested near Tern-9 in 2189. Not by us in the Annex; by the Authority, off the books. It was loud, it was high-power, and it left something behind on Tern-9's dish. Firmware 7 is not a diagnosis. It is a receipt for a story somebody decided to tell.

I do not know what was tested. I am calling it a weapon because that is the most expensive word I can think of, and the money is the part I can follow.

## Evidence for

- **Dish panel 7 appears on no purchase order.** It was fitted in the 2189 refit and booked as a "stores issue". Stores at Tern-9 do not carry dish panels. See [[Dish panel 7 (the one nobody ordered)]].
- **The frost was found beside panel 7**, and nowhere the dish was originally built.
- **The sector traffic log for Tern-9 has a 19-day gap** in autumn 2189. Gaps in Authority logs are approved in writing. I have asked to see the approval. The request has not been refused; it has not been answered.
- **B-01 blames the vendor too quickly.** The blocker went up the day after the empty-dish capture came back, and it points at paperwork, not at the echo. See [[B-01 Firmware 7 fault]].
- **Pellam's own report is missing** from every folder I can open. See [[Vendor audit]].

## Evidence against

- No direct record of any test. Everything above is absence: a missing order, a missing log, a missing report.
- A cover-up does not explain a reply that arrives before its message. Vesna is right about that, which I resent.
- The Authority has never run weapons. It has, however, run things it did not announce.

## What I want

The custody record for dish panel 7, and the approval for the 2189 log gap. If both are innocent, I will take this down myself.

Both are filed somewhere I cannot read. Hesper says the door is marked Classified and that I should stop knocking. I have not stopped. Start where I started: [[Dish panel 7 (the one nobody ordered)]].
