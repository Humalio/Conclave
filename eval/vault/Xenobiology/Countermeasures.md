# Countermeasures

What Xenobiology will do before, during and after a live listen to keep each keeper's dose and symptoms down. Owner: Linnea. Draft, 26 May 2191.

## Before the live listen

| When | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| T–72 h | Fixed sleep schedule; no extra shifts | Circadian stability |
| T–48 h to T–0 | Light schedule: bright light 06:00–08:00 station time, dim after 20:00 | Anchors body clock against the circadian phase |
| T–12 h | No alcohol, no sedatives | Avoids confounding vestibular tests |
| T–90 min | Lethorin 25 mg oral (REM suppressant) | Shortens the dream phase by ~30 % in volunteers |
| T–10 min | Bunk, harness, visor down | See [[Bunk shielding requirements]] |
| T–30 s | Head into restraint; eyes closed | Head motion is the largest controllable factor |

## During the live listen

- Eyes closed, visor down, head still.
- No speech except the keeper-in-charge's count and the second keeper's status.
- Keepers are told the count will run to zero and then a further five seconds. The listen ends when the keeper-in-charge says so, not when it feels as though it has ended.

## After the live listen

| When | Measure |
|---|---|
| T+0 to T+60 s | Remain in bunk; eyes closed |
| T+1 min | Keeper-in-charge and second keeper only open visors to begin the pulsar fix |
| T+10 min | Station physician checks every keeper: nystagmus, orientation, dream-recall log, dose badge |
| T+40 min | Keepers may leave bunks if oriented |
| T+0 to T+72 h | Light schedule resumes; melatonin onset tracked |

## What does not work

From volunteer trials: caffeine (worsens vertigo), fixed-gaze targets (no benefit), music (no benefit, though volunteers liked it). Lower-dose Lethorin (10 mg) had no measurable effect.

## Open questions

1. Lethorin was tested at p ≤ 0.30 only.
2. The second keeper must open their eyes first, before their dream phase is over. We are designing a seated pulsar-fix station so that they do not have to move their head.
3. If a corrective listen is needed (see [[Exposure limit]]), the second listen comes during the dream phase of the first. We have no data on that at all.
