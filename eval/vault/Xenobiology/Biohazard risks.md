# Biohazard risks

Xenobiology's risk register. Owner: Ayo. These are risks to the keepers' health; the hazards to the relay are in the [[Hazard log]]. Reviewed monthly; last review 28 May 2191.

## Scoring

Likelihood 1 (rare) to 5 (expected); impact 1 (discomfort) to 5 (death or permanent harm). Score = L × I. Scores of 12 and above go to the [[Risk register]] of the Echo Case.

## Commentary

**MR-01** is the risk that keeps this team up at night. Every keeper dose figure we publish is an extrapolation by a factor of 4.3 in power squared. The [[Exposure limit]] is honest about this; the schedule is less honest about how long it takes to get the data.

**MR-06.** The frost has been held in a sealed cell at 35 K since 10 April. No keeper has been exposed. The specimen is dormant above 60 K, and we have no evidence it can survive at body temperature. But "no evidence" is the phrase I keep writing about the frost, and it is starting to feel like a description of us rather than of the specimen. See [[Frost is a contaminant]].

**MR-07** was added after two keepers asked about "the second timestamp" during screening interviews. It was not from us. People talk.
