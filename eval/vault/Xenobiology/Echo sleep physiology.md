# Echo sleep physiology

What the early reply does to a sleeping person, as far as we can tell without having put a keeper through a full-power listen. Owner: Ayo (station physician). Last revised 19 May 2191.

## What echo sleep is

Echo sleep is a short disturbance of sleep and body-clock that follows sleeping near the dish while echoes arrive. Keepers hear the reply in dreams, a little before it arrives on screen. It is not an ordinary nightmare, though it shares some signs with one, and it is not jet lag, though it shares others. The working description, from 16 volunteers who slept in the shielded annex beside the dish test stand during low-power transmissions (power ≤ 0.30):

- **Dream phase (during sleep, and 0–40 min after waking).** A voice-like reply heard in the dream, then vertigo on eye closure, horizontal nystagmus, and a sensation that the floor is "half a step lower than it is". Onset within 30 s of the early reply.
- **Circadian phase (1–3 days).** Body-clock shift, measured by melatonin onset, of up to 2 h per exposure. Direction varies between subjects and has not been predicted.
- **Residual phase.** Six of 16 volunteers reported, in the first minute after waking, a brief sense of already knowing the next message. Not graded; recorded. None could repeat it.

## The unit

One **echo-hour (EH)** is the dose that produces, in the reference subject, 10 minutes of measurable nystagmus and a 40-minute melatonin-onset shift. Dose adds linearly within one exposure and decays with a half-life of about four days between exposures. That decay is why the [[Exposure limit]] has a 30-day term as well as a per-exposure term.

## What drives it

| Factor | Effect on dose | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Transmit power p | Rises with p² | Volunteer series, p 0.10–0.30 |
| Time on air | Linear | Volunteer series |
| Sleeping place | Open place ≈ 1.4 × shielded bunk | Bunk trials |
| Head motion at the moment of the reply | Raises dream-phase vertigo sharply | Bunk trials |
| Prior sleep debt | Lengthens circadian phase | Screening data |

The quantitative fit is the [[Dose model]].

## What we do not know

1. **Whether the p² law holds at p 0.62.** We have no human data above p 0.30, and we are not permitted to get any until the pause is lifted.
2. **Why the dream sometimes precedes the echo.** Two volunteers, on two separate runs, pressed the "dream" button before the test-stand receiver registered the echo. We put this down to anticipation. It may be. I do not like explanations that we reach for because the alternative is uncomfortable.
3. **Whether anything other than the signal contributes.** See [[Frost is a contaminant]].

## Consequences for design

The bunk exists because of the sleeping-place and head-motion rows above; see [[Bunk shielding requirements]]. The light schedule and pre-dosing exist because of the circadian phase; see [[Countermeasures]].

The volunteers were told the exposures were low-power and safe. They were. I would like to be able to say the same about the next ones.
