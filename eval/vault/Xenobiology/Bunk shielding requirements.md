# Bunk shielding requirements

What a shielded bunk shall do for the keeper in it. Owner: Ayo. Issued 22 April 2191 to Tern-9 Keepers, who turn it into the [[Bunk shielding spec]]. Controls hazards H-08 and H-10 in the [[Hazard log]].

## Why a shielded bunk

[[Echo sleep physiology]] shows two things that a bunk can change. Sleeping place: a keeper in a shielded, reclined bunk takes about 1.4 times less dose than one in an open sleeping place. Head motion at the moment of the reply sharply worsens the dream-phase vertigo. A shielded bunk is the cheapest dose reduction we have. It is also the place where a disoriented person cannot fall.

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| CR-1 | Each keeper (four now, five if the recruit proposal is approved) shall have a dedicated bunk. | Exposure limit applies per person |
| CR-2 | Recline 30° ± 3° from horizontal, head up. | Lowest dose in bunk trials |
| CR-3 | Head restraint shall limit motion to ± 2° in any axis from T–30 s to T+60 s about the send command. | Head motion drives dream-phase vertigo |
| CR-4 | Eyes-closed visor, opaque, keeper-operated. | Visual conflict worsens vertigo |
| CR-5 | Five-point harness, releasable by the occupant with one hand. | A disoriented keeper must still be able to leave |
| CR-6 | Bunk shall survive 4 g forward and 3 g lateral with occupant. | H-10, station docking shock |
| CR-7 | Dose badge holder at the sternum. | Running dose record |
| CR-8 | Bunk shall be oriented with the head away from the dish bay wall. | Leakage gradient (H-02) |
| CR-9 | Emesis bag and suction within reach. | It will happen |
| CR-10 | Bunks shall be at least 6 m from the receiver shield. | Leakage margin |

## Acceptance

Bunk prototypes shall be tested by Xenobiology with volunteers before installation. Tern-9 Keepers shall not install a bunk that Xenobiology has not accepted. Dunmore has agreed to this in writing, with an additional sentence about supply-drop time that is not reproduced here.

## Notes

The 30° recline was chosen over flat supine because three volunteers lying flat reported the heard-before-sent sensation more strongly. We do not understand that either, but the dose was also slightly higher, so the decision is easy.
