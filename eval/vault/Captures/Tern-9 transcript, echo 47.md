# Tern-9 transcript, echo 47

## Source

Intercom loop, Tern-9 dish test stand, receiver bay 2, 3 March 2191. Transcribed from the stand recorder by Lusa on 5 March. Timestamps are stand time, to 0.01 s where the recorder resolves it. Crosstalk and ventilation noise omitted. The bay microphone runs on its own audio archive, separate from [[live-listen-03.bin]], and is not transcribed here.

Speakers: **LUSA** (console), **TEODOR** (relay clock and send trigger; Signals Lab, on loan as clock keeper), **BEL** (Bel Arkwright, cryo bay, line of sight to the cold fixture). Three of Tern-9's four keepers were on the loop; the fourth was off shift.

Context: [[Echo 47 (why we stopped)]]. The transmission is live-listen test LL-03 in [[CAP-016 Live listen]].

## Notes

- The recorder kept running after the intercom lost power. It recorded nothing useful: the intercom was its only input.
- Intercom power was restored at 22:16. The loop log shows nobody spoke on it again that night.
- Lusa's transcription note, 5 March: "I have listened to the last four seconds about thirty times. Bel is not imagining the ticking. It is on the loop, under her voice, before the reply. I don't know what it is."
