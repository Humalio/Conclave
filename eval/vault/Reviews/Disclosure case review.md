# Disclosure case review

Review RV-09 of the [[Disclosure case]], revision B. Held 28 April 2191. Chair: Marguerite. Reviewers: Ezra, Hollis; outside physician for §7. Presenter: Saoirse (Case Command). Minutes by Quentin.

## Scope

All eight sections of revision B, against the [[Disclosure basis]] and the [[Review checklist]]. The review was not asked to assess readiness for disclosure, only whether the case is structured to reach it.

## Findings

| # | Grade | Section | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| S-1 | **Major** | §4 | The explanation claim rests on transmissions at power 0.25 and a timestamp fault model for the clock path only. Neither covers operating power. Claim shall be marked UNSUPPORTED until CAP-014 transmissions 201–800 complete. |
| S-2 | **Major** | §6 | The evidence base contains an unexplained timing event (echo 47, 0.71 s early). §6 shall state that it does not rely on any explanation, and the Board shall not close §6 while it is unexplained. |
| S-3 | **Major** | §7 | The echo-hour model is extrapolated by a factor of 4.3 in exposure. State the extrapolation explicitly; Xenobiology to provide a data plan. |
| S-4 | **Major** | §8 | H-12, raised 23 April, has no cause, no controls beyond isolation and no verification. Residual risk shall be stated as "cannot be assessed". |
| S-5 | Minor | §1 | Scope excludes the other thirty-nine relays; add a cross-reference to the Net service rules. |
| S-6 | Minor | §2 | Cite the Refit plan for the station configuration. |
| S-7 | Minor | §3 | State that the hazard log governs where the case disagrees. |
| S-8 to S-13 | Minor | various | Editorial. |

All findings incorporated in revision C (draft, 1 June), except S-3, where Xenobiology's data plan awaits the lifting of the power cap.

## Discussion

Saoirse proposed that §6 be closed on the strength of [[CAP-011 Clock holdover]], which rules out jitter. Hollis objected: ruling out one cause does not explain the event. Ezra agreed. Saoirse withdrew the proposal.

Marguerite asked whether any member of the case held information about echo 47 that had not been presented. Saoirse said she was not aware of any. Hollis asked that the question and answer be minuted verbatim. They were.

## Outcome

Revision B not accepted. Revision C to be prepared for review in August. The case shall be resubmitted with every major finding addressed.

## Chair's closing remark

"A disclosure case is an argument that the public can be told something true. This one is, at present, an honest argument that we do not yet know what is true. I prefer that to the other kind." — Marguerite
