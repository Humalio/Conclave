# Whiteboard, 14 March

Photograph of the whiteboard in the Signals Lab meeting room on Corvel Station, taken by Sef at 17:52 on Friday 14 March 2191, at the end of the echo 47 post-capture review. No minutes were filed for this review. The photograph is the only record. Described here in text because the image file was lost when the meeting-room tablet was reset on 17 March.

## What the photograph shows

The board is a standard 2 m wall board, photographed slightly from the left, so the right edge is foreshortened. Four people wrote on it, in four pens: black, blue, red and a green that is nearly dry.

**Left third, black pen, neat columns.** Two columns of times headed "SEND" and "RX-2". Under SEND: 22:14:07.000, 22:14:07.000, 22:14:07.000, written three times as if someone kept checking. Under RX-2: 22:14:06.290, then 06.291, then 06.290 again. A line drawn across under them, and the difference written beneath: **0.710**. Beside the columns, smaller: "clock A", "clock B", "both".

**Centre, blue pen.** A sketch of Tern-9's dish, seen face on, with the power readback drawn across it: flat at 0.25, a hump to 0.34, flat again. "3.0 s" under the hump. An arrow from the start of the hump back to the left columns. Above the hump, in the same hand: "plan 0.25 / got 0.34 / WHY".

**Centre, red pen, circled three times.** "**0.71 s ??**". The three circles are not concentric; the third is drawn hard enough that the pen has skipped. Two question marks, the second larger than the first.

**Right third, blue and black, a list.**
- jitter? (in black; ticked, then the tick crossed out)
- ground loop? (crossed out)
- log write order? (crossed out)
- dish mic ticking = same lead? (underlined twice)
- ~~ask the director~~ (struck through with a single firm line, red pen)

**Bottom right corner, green pen, small.** "**2.3G?**" with an arrow pointing off the edge of the board. Nobody has admitted to writing it. The green pen was found dry in the tray the following Monday.

**Bottom centre, black pen.** A doodle: a single short vertical tick mark, drawn over and over in a row, about thirty of them, each slightly leaning, like a tally that does not stop at five.

**Top right, someone else's hand.** "Do NOT wipe" and, underneath, wiped partly away, "— T".

## What can be read from it

1. Both relay clock inputs logged the reply: clock A logged the send command at 07.000, and clock B logged the reply arriving at 06.290, 0.71 s earlier. "both" is written beside the columns.
2. The dish-house microphone's ticking was thought at the time to share the same lead.
3. A receiver ring near 2.3 GHz was noticed by someone, who did not put it in any filed note.
4. Someone proposed asking the Director, and the proposal was withdrawn in the room.
5. The jitter hypothesis was accepted, then un-accepted, before the photograph was taken.

## Afterwards

Teodor's notebook for the following week records the cause as clock jitter. Imani's does not. [[CAP-011 Clock holdover]] ruled out jitter on 21 March.

The board was wiped on 17 March. The tick marks were the last thing to go; whoever cleaned it left them for a day.

See [[Second timestamp]] for what the relay clocks recorded, and [[Echo 47 (why we stopped)]] for what happened after.
