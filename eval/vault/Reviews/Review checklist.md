# Review checklist

The checklist every review package shall pass before it is issued, and every reviewer shall work through before the meeting. Maintained by Quentin (Case Command) under [[Independent review rules]]. Revision 3, 24 April 2191.

## Before issue (presenter)

- [ ] Package issued at least five working days before the review.
- [ ] Every note in the package is at status active or frozen, or the draft status is declared on the cover.
- [ ] Every claim carries a capture ID (R11), or is marked UNSUPPORTED.
- [ ] Every result cites the capture's data file, not a summary of it.
- [ ] The requirements the item satisfies are listed by number ([[Requirements]] R1–R12).
- [ ] The hazards the item controls are listed by ID ([[Hazard log]]).
- [ ] Assumptions are listed, with the power range in which each has been tested.
- [ ] Open questions are listed. A package with no open questions shall be returned to the presenter.
- [ ] Reviewers named, with their independence stated.

## During review (reviewer)

- [ ] Does each claim follow from its evidence, or only sit next to it?
- [ ] Is any model used outside the range where it was tested?
- [ ] Are summary figures consistent with the raw data? (Check at least one raw file.)
- [ ] Does any evidence contradict another piece of evidence in the package?
- [ ] Is anything missing that a reasonable reviewer would expect to see?
- [ ] Is there reason to think relevant evidence exists that has not been presented? (Record as an observation; see Independent review rules 5.2.)

## After review (chair)

- [ ] Findings graded major, minor or observation.
- [ ] Every major finding has an owner and a date.
- [ ] Minutes filed within two working days.
- [ ] Entry made in the [[Review log]].

## Notes on revision 3

Two lines were added on 24 April.

"Check at least one raw file" was added because echo summaries average over 1 s, and a reviewer reading only summaries cannot see anything shorter, or anything that arrived before it was sent. Mirela proposed it. She did not say what prompted it.

"Is there reason to think relevant evidence exists that has not been presented?" was added after RV-05, the echo 47 review, which produced a whiteboard and no minutes. Hollis proposed it. He said exactly what prompted it.
