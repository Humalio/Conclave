## The question nobody asked out loud

The frost did not grow on Tern-9's dish for two years while the face was warm. It woke when we cooled it to 40 K on a dish that answers. HX-2's fold went through the substrate for eleven seconds, and panel 7 was the nearest cold surface.

If the frost formed where the substrate was touched ([[What answers us]]), then it is not a contaminant that happens to like our dish. It is a native of whatever the fold test reached, and Tern-9 is the first place on this side where it can hear home.

## Custody now

Sealed cell SC-1 at 35 K in the Tern-9 dish house. Xenobiology asked twice to move it to the Corvel cold store; Signals Lab wants it where it can hear the dish. It has stayed. Two-person rule for opening. No sample has left Tern-9. R12 bars uncharacterised biological material from the relay loop.
