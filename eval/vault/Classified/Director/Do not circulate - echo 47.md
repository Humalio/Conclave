# Do not circulate - echo 47

**DO NOT CIRCULATE.** Distribution: Director, Signals Lab lead, Assistant Director. 4 March 2191, 07:50 Corvel time.

## Order

With effect from now:

1. No transmission from any relay, test stand or service, shall exceed transmit power **0.30**. Interlock limits on Tern-9 to be set to 0.30 by 12:00 today. Every other relay by 18:00.
2. Live listen LL-05 is postponed without a date.
3. The LL-03 page of the live-listen log (echo 47) is withdrawn from the shared folders and replaced by a redaction notice. The raw recording stays on the Tern-9 archive under its file name.
4. Questions about echo 47 come to me, not to the review board, and not to the keepers.

## Reason

Echo 47 was profiled at power 0.25. The readback says the dish held 0.34 for three seconds, and nobody commanded it. The relay clock logged the reply twice; the first entry is 0.71 s *before* the send command existed. Every receiver on the dish rang at 2.3 GHz from the first entry, outside the traffic band. The bay microphone recorded ticking from 22:14:05.58, which is 0.71 s before the reply arrived.

Teodor read the reply off the screen before the message had gone, and said so. The transcript stops there.

Until we can say what arrived first, we shall not go back above 0.30. I would rather lose a quarter than lose the Net's word.

## Why it is not on the review board

Because I do not yet know whether it is a fault in our instruments or something I already have reason to fear, and the second one changes what the Authority is. If it is instrumentation, Timekeepers will find it by the end of the month and this memo can be burned. If it is not, I will write it up properly myself.

*Timekeepers ruled out the clock on 21 March. I wrote it up on 31 May: [[What answers us]].*

## Who has seen this

Orla (Signals Lab), Saoirse (Case Command). Lusa and Teodor were at the console; they know the campaign was stopped, not why the order went this far. Bel Arkwright was in the Tern-9 cryo bay and heard the ticking. Nobody has asked her about it since, and she has not offered.
