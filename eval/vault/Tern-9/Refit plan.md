# Refit plan

How the four keepers and the refit crews turn a 2171 relay into one with a rebuilt dish array without breaking it. Owner: Dunmore. Revision 3, 26 May 2191. Target: M5 "Tern-9 refit complete", 15 December.

## The station we've got

Tern-9: 140 m, four keepers, commissioned 2171, reactor aft, receiver mast along the spine, living quarters sized for 22 during a refit. Station mass on our scales at the start of refit on 27 January: **6,850 t**. She's a good relay. She was built honest and she's been run hard, and every frame has a story nobody wrote down.

R8 says the refit adds no more than 6 % of station mass. That's **411 t**. We have to fit a dish array, a power train and a sleeper-protection liner into 411 t and a tower that was drawn for something else.

## Sequence

1. **Strip and survey** (Feb–Jun). Open the tower between stations 38 and 52. Pull skin, survey every frame, log what we find. Done mostly; see the Station log, and the one panel everybody asks about: [[Dish panel 7 (the one nobody ordered)]].
2. **Structure** (Jul–Aug, plus one week in Oct). Forgings arrive 14 July. Stubs and doublers first. The frame 44 web cut and insert wait for the ground live listen (M4, 30 Sep): we don't cut a frame for an array that hasn't transmitted. [[Dish mount installation]].
3. **Power train** (Aug–Oct). Bank modules in four lots from 1 August. Converters, dump, cable.
4. **Shroud** (Sep–Oct). 24 segments from the Corvel works, scarf-bonded in place.
5. **Feeds** (Nov). Backing ring closes, twelve horns hung.
6. **Living volume** (Sep–Nov). Liner, bunks.
7. **Close-out** (Dec). Skin back on, systems check, hand to Case Command.

The detail lives in [[Work packages]] and [[Refit schedule]]. The lifts live in [[Supply drop plan]].

## What keeps me up

- **Shroud qualification.** We're cutting shroud metal into the schedule before M3 says the explanation is qualified. If [[CAP-014 Air-gapped capture]] goes badly for Firmware 7, the shroud slot moves and everything behind it moves with it.
- **Conductor.** Released at risk; reels due 22 August. Horns need nine days each on two formers. We have no float on that line.
- **The tower.** Every frame we open has a surprise. So far: two corroded rivet rows, a cable nobody can trace, and panel 7.
