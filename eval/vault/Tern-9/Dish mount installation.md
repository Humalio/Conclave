# Dish mount installation

How the keepers put in the new mount structure that The Annex designed. Owner: Rook. Revision 2, 28 May 2191. Design is in [[Dish mount reinforcement]]; this is the method.

## Scope

- 24 mount stubs (Ti-6-4 forgings, 408 kg each).
- Doubler sets on frames 41 to 47.
- Frame 44 web insert, stations 3–9.
- Removal of the old instrument-bay mount on frame 44.

## Before we touch anything

1. **Survey every frame.** Ultrasonic thickness at 200 mm pitch, dye-penetrant on every original weld we can see. Results to the [[Station log]]. Done for frames 38–46; 47–52 by 13 June.
2. **Shore the tower.** With the skin off, she sags. Eight hydraulic shores under the tower line between stations 36 and 50, set to the survey heights from January. Check daily. We've seen 1.4 mm of movement at frame 44 since skin-off; within limits, watched.
3. **Clean.** Every doubler face back to bright metal, solvent wipe, primer within four hours. If the primer sits longer than four hours, sand it back and start again. No arguments.

## Sequence per frame

| Step | Time | Who | Hold point |
|---|---|---|---|
| Template frame, drill pilot holes | 1 shift | Rook's fitters | template inspection |
| Bond doubler, clamp, cure 24 h | 2 shifts | bonders | cure log |
| Drill to final, ream, rivet | 2 shifts | fitters | 10 % rivet pull test |
| Fit stubs (3 or 4 per frame; 2 on frame 44) | 1 shift | fitters + crane | torque log |
| NDT | 1 shift | inspection | C-scan clean |

Frame 44 adds two shifts for the insert. Seven frames, roughly 51 shifts, three shifts a day: **17 working days** if nothing goes wrong. We've scheduled 22 for the doublers and stubs in July and August, and a separate week in October for frame 44.

## The M4 rule

Doublers and stubs are bonded, riveted and bolted onto frames we leave whole. The frame 44 insert is different: it means cutting out a section of original web between stations 3 and 9. Tern-9 does not cut a tower frame until the ground live listen (M4, due 30 September) has passed. If the array never transmits, we can take the doublers off and give the Authority back the station they lent us. We cannot give them back a frame web. So frame 44 is cut in week 41, not before. Nobody is energising a feed in Berth 3 in the meantime, so the +0.06 margin only has to hold the tower up, which it does.

## No welding

No welding within 2 m of original structure. Her 2171 weld records are incomplete, and I'm not putting heat into a joint I can't prove. Bonded and riveted only. The Annex agrees; this is in the design.

## Access

Once the backing ring goes on, stub roots are only visible through the aft-facing inspection ports. So every stub gets a photographed, signed inspection before the ring lifts, and the photos go to the Station log. If you're the one doing it, take more photos than you think.

- Crane slots: Berth 3 gantry, booked 15 July to 8 August; see [[Supply drop plan]].
- Cable trays on frames 41 and 47 go in after the doublers, not before; see [[Cable routing]].
