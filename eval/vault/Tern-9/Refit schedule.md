## Float

Float to M5: **six working days**, all at the end. Horns come off the formers in early November if the conductor reels land on 22 August. That's the critical path, and it's not even ours: it runs through [[Feed horn spec]] and a reel tender we've never met.

Second-worst path: the shroud. WP-06 starts 1 September on the assumption that M3 passes on 31 July. If [[CAP-014 Air-gapped capture]] or anything else moves M3, the shroud slot moves, the horns can't close the ring around a shroud that isn't there, and the six days go in an afternoon.

## Bunks

The bunk slot (weeks 37–40) trails the screened liner zone by zone on purpose: we don't fit bunks in a room we're about to line. If Xenobiology changes [[Bunk shielding requirements]], the slot stays and the work inside it changes. If they change it after 8 September, we pay.

## Things the schedule doesn't show

- Berth 3 is ours to 31 January 2192. After that, a tanker tender is booked in. There is no slipping past January without moving a tender, and I've moved tenders before. It costs.
- Holidays: the station stands down 24–26 December. Not negotiable; I've tried.
- Night shift on the tower only. Day shift is noisy enough.

## Changes

- Rev 6, 9 May: bunks moved from weeks 41–43 to 37–40 to get out of the shroud's way.
- Rev 7, 30 May: WP-06 start held at 1 Sep pending CAP-014 at 500 transmissions (due 3 June).
