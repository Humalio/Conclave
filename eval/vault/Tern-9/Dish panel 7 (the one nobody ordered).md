# Dish panel 7 (the one nobody ordered)

Written by Dunmore, 31 May 2191, because I have now been asked about this panel by four teams, two of them twice, and I'd like to answer once.

## The panel

Panel 7 is an inner ring dish panel, stations 41 to 43, port of the feed axis. Titanium-backed, 2.4 m by 1.6 m, 22 mm thick. It looks like the other forty-three. It isn't.

It is not Firmware 7. Firmware 7 is the build the Authority blames. Panel 7 is a panel. If I had a credit for every time someone in a meeting said "seven" and meant the other one, I'd buy the station.

## Where it should have come from

Tern-9 was commissioned in 2171 with forty-four panels, numbered from the build drawings. In 2189 the dish ring had a nine-week refit and some panels were reconditioned or swapped. I went through every order from that refit: purchase orders, the stores ledger, the freight manifests. Forty-three panels are accounted for, with a supplier plate on the back and a line in the ledger.

Panel 7 has no supplier plate. It has a serial stamped in a format we have never used. It is on **no purchase order**. The 2189 refit notes say only: "inner ring 7, replaced, source: Authority stores". There is no Authority stores number. Somebody fitted a panel nobody ordered and wrote it down as if it had always been there.

## Why it came off, and where it went

The feed ring goes in at stations 41 to 47. Every inner ring panel in that span had to come off for the refit survey, so panel 7 came off on **14 February** with the rest. Normal job. One tug, two fitters, Rook swearing at a seized fastener.

Normally a panel with no paperwork goes to scrap. This one didn't. Test Stand 2 asked the same week for titanium fixture stock, flat, thick, cold-rated, and panel 7 was the right gauge sitting on the berth floor. So it went over on request, and they cut the **backing plate for the cold fixture in Cold bay 2** out of it, the plate the capture receivers bolt onto. The offcut came back to rack 9. It's all in the [[Station log]].

At the time, that was me being thrifty. I've since been told it was me being *interesting*, and I liked thrifty better.

## The odd bit

Before it left, the cleaners did the panel the way they do every panel: degrease, wipe, polish out surface marks so the test stand gets clean stock. The cold face, the one that looks at the receivers, had a sort of **frost** on it: fine, milky, like breath on a cold window, in a patch the size of a hand. Pads came off clean. Solvent came off clean. Third pass, the bloom was still there, exactly where it was. The cleaning note says "surface discolouration, not removable, accept as-is". That's what I signed.

Ottilie put her bare hand flat on it, because Ottilie does that, and said it felt cold. The shed was at 19 °C. The panel had been in it for six hours. I'm not putting that in a report. (I just did.)

## What happened after

- **26–27 Feb.** Test Stand 2 cuts the backing plate, bloom and all. Offcut back in rack 9.
- **9 Apr.** Cold bay 2 cryo alarm. Night technician finds a film on one of the receivers sitting on that fixture.
- **10–11 Apr.** Test Stand 2, then Xenobiology, ask for the full history of panel 7: treatments, coatings, chemicals, cleaning, since 2189. Sent what we had.
- **23 Apr.** Somebody, reference not given, has the offcut in rack 9 bagged and sealed. My fitters are told not to open rack 9.
- **6 May.** Test Stand 2 asks for a second fixture backing plate for their new Cold bay 3. Fresh stock. Their words: *no panel-7 material*. Nobody says why. Nobody had to.

## What I don't know

I don't know what was in that bloom. I don't know whether it came with the panel or woke up on the fixture once it was cold. I don't know why it went cold in a warm shed. And I don't know where the panel was before 2189, because the paperwork behind "Authority stores" goes to the Authority, not the station.

Somebody does. I asked the case for the panel's chain of custody from before 2189 to my berth. I was told it exists and that it's filed under [[Frost chain of custody]]. I can't open it. Neither can anyone I've asked.

So: the panel's plate is in a test bay, the offcut's in a sealed bag in my rack, and the thing on the receiver has a catalogue number. Start at [[IR-0412 Tern-9 cryo alarm]]. And one more thing, which isn't in any log: last night Ottilie stood in front of rack 9 for a minute. The bag ticks. She counted. It ticked, and 0.7 seconds later the reply light on the console came on.

Nobody had sent anything.
