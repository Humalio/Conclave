## Package rules

1. **One lead per package.** If two people own it, nobody does.
2. **Every package closes with a signed record** in the Station log: what went in, serials, torque logs, photographs, deviations.
3. **Deviations go to the designer the same shift.** The station does not decide that a bracket 4 mm off is fine. The Annex or Relay Engineering decides, in writing.
4. **No package starts on a drawing older than its design note.** If [[Dish mount reinforcement]] moves to Rev C, WP-03 waits for the Rev C drawings.

## Interfaces that bite

- **WP-03 → WP-07.** Trays on frames 41 and 47 hang off the doublers. Cable can't go in before structure.
- **WP-06 → WP-04b.** The horn ring closes round the shroud. If the shroud is late, the horns sit in their crates.
- **WP-09 → WP-10.** Bunks follow liner, zone by zone.
- **WP-02 → everyone.** Removals free the space. The frame 44 mount is the last big removal.
