## Conventions

- Panel numbers follow the 2171 build drawings, inner ring first. Panel 7 is a panel, not a firmware build. Firmware 7 is a build, not a panel. This has been confused in at least four meetings.
- "On request" means someone outside the station asked and the keepers did it. The request is filed with the entry.
- Nothing is deleted. Wrong entries get a correction line.
