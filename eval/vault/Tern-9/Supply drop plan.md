# Supply drop plan

Lifts, berthing and supply drops for Tern-9 during the refit. Owner: Rook. Revision 4, 24 May 2191.

## Berth 3

Tern-9's Berth 3 is a pressurised dock, 180 m by 46 m, with the station held on six strongbacks and four lateral clamps. Half-gravity spin section above the berth for the refit crews; the berth floor itself is in zero-g, which matters for every lift below. The nearest other human is eleven light-hours away, so everything arrives in a supply drop and nothing is ordered twice.

- Refit started 27 January, supply tenders berth bow toward the doors.
- Strongbacks at stations 10, 30, 56, 76, 100, 124. None at the tower; we kept it clear on purpose.
- Lateral clamps at stations 30 and 100.
- Shores under the tower since skin-off: eight hydraulic, stations 36–50; see [[Dish mount installation]].

## Cranes

| Crane | Type | Capacity | Reach | Name on the berth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | overhead gantry, full berth length | 60 t | whole station | "Old Mag" |
| G2 | overhead gantry, aft half | 25 t | stations 40–140 | "Gull" |
| T1 | tether tug, free-flying | 8 t | anywhere | none |
| J1–J4 | jib cranes at the tower platforms | 2 t | local | none |

In zero-g, capacity is about momentum, not weight. Our limits are the gantry's stopping force and how fast a load can be moved without it carrying on into the station. Rule: no load moves faster than 0.1 m/s within 5 m of the dish structure. Every heavy lift has a tag line at each end and a tug standing by.

## Berth services

- Power: 2 MW from the station grid. Enough to run the hotel loads and the keepers' tools; not enough to charge a bank. Bank testing in berth uses one string at a time.
- Cryo: the station's helium plant can feed Loop A for leak tests only.
- Hazard: no feed energised in Berth 3, ever. Ramping happens after the refit, from the transmit hall.
