# The Last Playback topology

Two teams share one case. **The Last Playback** argues what should happen to the recording. **The Listening Room** checks what the recording can establish. Vela connects the two as the independent reviewer.

| Seat | Team | Work |
|---|---|---|
| Arden · Chair | The Last Playback | Convene the hearing and record the decision and dissent. |
| Ilex · Estate counsel | The Last Playback | Argue that the human record should be withdrawn. |
| Nacre · Witness counsel | The Last Playback | Argue that the robot can keep an account of the shared act. |
| Vela · Examiner | Both teams | Review citations and test the strongest argument on each side. |
| Tess · Evidence clerk | The Listening Room | Mark the boundary between the transcript and a witness's interpretation. |

In **Agents → Topology**, use **All** for the team links and **Projects** for the opening view. Open **The Last Playback** to show the two briefs, then **The Listening Room** for the evidence check. The nodes show actual connection state. They become active when the corresponding conversations log in.

For the short take, connect Arden, Ilex, Nacre and Vela. Tess is optional. The preloaded WSL hub already has the teams and assignments. See the [operator runbook](runbook.md) and [promo storyboard](promo-storyboard.md).
