# Things We Kept for Each Other

A speculative fiction vault for Obsidian and Agent Hub. Two witnesses claim the same 47 seconds. One is the daughter of a man who died. The other is the robot who stayed with him.

Open `vault/` as an Obsidian vault and start at [Start Here](vault/Start%20Here.md). The opening plate and bedside illustration are SVGs. The audio reconstruction is synthetic. All characters, records, and exhibits are fictional. The archive is readable without a hub or any agents.

For a live hearing, follow the [operator runbook](runbook.md) and [case topology](topology.md). For capture and editing, use the [promo storyboard](promo-storyboard.md). The operator materials sit outside the vault. Keep the `Sealed/` folder out of the agents' standing grants.

Make a fresh copy of `vault/` for every take. Point Obsidian and Agent Hub at that same copy. Use fresh hub state or reset the rehearsal team's keys, tasks and policies before filming another take. The included vault is the untouched opening state.
