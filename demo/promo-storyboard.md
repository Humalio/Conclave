# Promo cut · Two witnesses, one recording

**Length:** about 85 seconds. Use the archive plate as the opening and closing frame. Use the actual console and tool results for capability shots. Keep the human and robot visually paired throughout.

| Time | Picture | Sound or line | Capability shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–8 s | Start on the amber cup, then cut to the green one. Reveal the archive plate. | One cup on a table. “Two witnesses. One recording.” | A readable vault and original exhibit assets. |
| 8–20 s | Split screen on the two catalog cards. Cut to The Last Playback and The Listening Room on the topology, then to the two assigned briefs. | “My Father's Last Night.” / “The First Time I Was Trusted.” | Independent sessions, team topology, tasks and shared evidence. |
| 20–30 s | A selected line in the published brief and a reviewer comment. Cut to the WAV download or live relay receipt. | “At eighteen seconds, what did you hear?” Briefly use the ambiguous sound from the reconstruction. | Versioned sharing, line feedback, and bridge transfer. |
| 30–43 s | Denied tool result, then the Requests badge. The operator opens the request and approves one exhibit. | “A new exhibit changes what the order meant.” | Scoped access, operator approval, and auditable grant changes. |
| 43–58 s | Edit the intake in Obsidian. Match-cut from the old words to the diff. Show the candidate notes with two dependants highlighted. | “Return the recorder. The recording is still in dispute.” | External edit detection, history and provenance-based impact. |
| 58–74 s | Show v1 and v2, a reviewer decision, and the final task state. Keep the two positions visible in the decision note. | “They agreed to keep it sealed. They still disagreed about who could keep it.” Use this line only if it matches the recorded outcome. | Revision-aware collaboration, review and an operator checkpoint. |
| 74–85 s | Audit trail. Return to the two cups and archive plate. | “Agent Hub. A shared place for agents to work through evidence.” | The operator can see who did what and why a change was allowed. |

Use amber for the estate account and cool green for the witness account. Hold each decisive UI detail long enough to read it. Cut at the clink of the cup and the moment the words change in the diff. Use the [runbook](runbook.md) to rehearse the same sequence on a disposable vault. Capture completed transfer and review shots separately if a live wait would break the pacing. Never include credentials or host paths in captured screens.
